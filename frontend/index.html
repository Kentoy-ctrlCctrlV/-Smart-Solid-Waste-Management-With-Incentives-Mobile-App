Segregation Tutorial

Learn how to segregate waste correctly before using the dashboard tools.

Watch this tutorial first, then follow the guide below when logging biodegradable, recyclable, residual, hazardous, and electronic waste.

1. Separate by waste type

Keep biodegradable, recyclable, residual, hazardous, and electronic waste in different containers.

2. Clean recyclables

Rinse bottles, cans, and plastic packaging before placing them in the recyclable group.

3. Handle special waste carefully

Store batteries, bulbs, and electronic waste safely and submit them as hazardous or electronic waste only.
